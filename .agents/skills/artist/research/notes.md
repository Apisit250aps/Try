## Questions and accepted evidence

| Research question | Source observation | Local synthesis / where applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does visual development deliver? | A01 connects theme, character/world look and emotional moments; A03 distinguishes exploration from selected model sheets. | Distinguish mood exploration, design selection and reproducible production construction. Route new-look work to visual development and sheets to character guidance. |
| How to avoid generic “good design” advice? | A05 distinguishes shape/form and hue/value/intensity; A06 describes emphasis and visual relationships. | Choose primary read, use-size tests and cause-specific corrections. These tests and rubric thresholds are local heuristics, not museum rules. |
| Is shape language a character taxonomy? | A07's final page explicitly describes exceptions and mixed shapes. | Treat shapes as hypotheses in context; prohibit inferring moral/demographic essence. Test cast distinction without hue alone. |
| How to balance gesture and construction? | A08 builds connected volumes along gesture; A09 judges pose meaning as well as silhouette. | Test support, contact and action before contour/detail. Preserve intentional stylization; do not mistake every distortion for an error or accept every anatomy error as style. |
| What is a useful expression study? | A10 treats face posing as structured performance and checks gaze from camera. | Keep construction/landmarks while changing action; “focused versus angry” is a diagnostic example, not a scientific emotion standard. Leave acting timing/lip sync to other roles. |
| What makes costume part of a character? | A11 begins with script, research and collaborators. | Check closures, routing, reach and variants against the supplied world/action. Movement tests are proposed production checks, not garment certification. |
| How should a space support production? | A04 plans reusable backgrounds and foreground/background interaction; A12 distinguishes projections and parallel-edge families. | Test routes, landmarks, scale and view-to-view geography before detail. ## Evidence strength and rejected simplifications

Institutional job profiles are strong evidence of responsibilities, weak evidence
of a specific drawing method. Museum lessons are useful foundations, not a full
professional curriculum. Practitioner lessons explain craft, with stylistic
judgment rather than universal experimental proof. Tool manuals support mechanics,
not artistic success. License/standards documents support their actual scope,
not a general clearance or compliance claim.

Do not carry Getty's introductory pigment-primary simplification into digital RGB
as color science. Do not turn the shape guide into “triangles are evil.” Do not
turn advice about readable poses into a requirement to move a locked camera or
to force every performance into theatrical exaggeration. Do not copy Disney's
film palettes or proprietary pipeline as project requirements.

The following are **local**: basic-style defaults; pixel minimums and palette
planning budgets; two corrective attempts; rubric score thresholds; concise
handoff fields; current role split and creation roadmap. They are not attributed
to external sources. Existing image-model continuity/alpha caveats are production
constraints to verify, not performance claims established by these research pages.

## Research limits and next evidence

No paywalled textbook/course, full video curriculum, species-anatomy treatise,
costume fabrication manual or native 3D pipeline was consumed. No new copyrighted
artwork was copied into the package. Asset-specific anatomy, culture, period and
technical destinations still need targeted research when a brief requires them.
The source set supplies an attributable foundation and actionable checks; it does
not establish professional mastery or replace a human art director's judgment.

Next research should be driven by observed trials: cross-view volume drift,
subtle expression readability, repeatable lighting/material matching, and reference
rights uncertainty. Add a source only if it changes a decision or addresses an
observed gap. Recheck changing tool/license/standards behavior against the owning
source. See [audit](audit.md) and [scenarios](../tests/scenarios.md) for proof gaps.
