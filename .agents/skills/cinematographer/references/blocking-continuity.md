# Blocking, spatial grammar and continuity

Use for dialogue, action, reveal coverage and deliberate discontinuity. The
classical continuity system is a convention, not physics or a rule for all cinema.
Claim IDs: [P03/P06](../research/notes.md). The maps/checks here are local H/S02.

## Build the space that the cut needs

Record fixed landmarks, participants/props, entrance/exit, positions, facing and
action routes. Use world coordinates or an unambiguous plan with units; define
screen left/right separately. Blocking is movement/position through action;
staging arranges its readability in the chosen frame. Discuss performance changes
with Director, script-action changes with Screenwriter. Don't move a crucial prop
into a character's reach if the inability to reach it causes the scene. [S01/H]

For each beat test a key pose and the transition: can the audience see the cause,
contact and result? A perfect still silhouette can fail when a hand overlaps a face.
Compare camera relocation, actor staggering, changed timing and an insert; choose
the least costly option preserving performance and intent. [H]

## Two-person dialogue geometry

Example local map: A at (-1,0), B at (1,0), y increases north. A–B is the x-axis;
primary cameras remain at y<0. Establish A screen-left, B screen-right. A's look
to B reads screen-right; B's return reads screen-left. For A single place camera
near B on the south side; for B single near A on the south side. Match look-target
height and distance, not just a vague “eyes right.” A tiny off-camera target near
the lens and a far-side partner imply different relationships. [H worked geometry]

| Plan | Why / when | Cost or defect → remedy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared master/two shot | Establish table, positions and simultaneous behavior. | If it reveals the secret too soon, restrict framing/occlusion without losing needed geography. |
| Matched singles / OTS | Let a line or reaction shift attention while preserving partner position. | Both looking screen-right suggests different targets. Re-check camera side and target; don't flip an asymmetric character image to fake a match. |
| Listening room | Cover the listener from before stimulus through a usable response/settle. | Continuous cutting on speaker changes removes the response. Give Editor a held reaction and its trigger. |
| Prop interaction | Record actor's own left/right hand, object face, contact and location. | A token teleports into the other hand. Restore state or mark an explicit transfer/ellipsis; an insert alone does not excuse it. |

## Axes, 180 and 30-degree principles

The 180-degree convention keeps cameras on one side of an axis to help maintain
screen relationships. The active axis may change with moving participants or
attention; draw the new line and show the audience how it changed. It is distinct
from shutter angle. The 30-degree guideline suggests a substantial angular change
between similar successive views of the same subject to avoid a near-duplicate
jump. It is a craft convention, not a universal threshold; a large size change,
cutaway, intentional jump or uninterrupted shot may be the better choice.
Do not claim a 31° cut is guaranteed smooth. [P06/P23: C]

For a proposed axis crossing state the purpose, bridge, new orientation and
recovery. Options include a continuous visible camera arc, actor movement that
establishes a new line, or a clear re-establishing view. An arbitrary insert may
interrupt the cut but need not teach the new geography. [H]

**Intentional example:** Director wants the negotiation's turning point to change
our relation to both people. Maintain A-left/B-right for the opening, then arc
continuously from south to north while keeping both people and the fixed door
visible. Pass through a briefly overlapping/profile view, settle A-right/B-left,
then take all following singles from north. No character swaps seats. Compare
against a simple south-side wide if the arc distracts from performance. If the
goal is actual disorientation, label its extent and how/when clarity returns;
still preserve world state for the makers. See T09 in
[worked sequences](../examples/worked-sequences.md). [H]

## Continuity is several ledgers

| Track | Question / diagnostic (H) | Repair / deliberate exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spatial | Does the look, exit or motion lead where the next view says it leads? | Re-establish the route; keep a landmark through a move. Don't mirror a frame that reverses costume/prop identity. |
| Temporal | Are adjacent shots continuous, overlapping, ellipsed or simultaneous? | Name omitted/repeated time and evidence for it; do not add every alternate's duration to the cut total. |
| Action | Do hand, foot, contact and body phase match at the proposed cut? | Cover matching phases with handles or deliberately repeat/omit for emphasis with Editor. |
| State / identity | Are character design, costume, wetness, token orientation and damage consistent? | Use asset versions and explicit state changes; regenerate a drifting shot rather than silently revise canon. |
| Light / environment | Do source direction, weather, time and practical states connect? | Match world sources; document any lighting cheat or time jump. |
| Knowledge / reveal | Did a shot show the clue before its allowed beat? Can the character see it too? | Reframe/occlude or move the disclosure within authorized order; escalate if script intent conflicts. |

Cut on a movement because it supports the inference/performance, not to conceal
every mismatch. For fast action, preserve destination, direction and consequence
before increasing cut frequency. For calm scenes, continuity of attention may
matter more than perfectly repeated gestures; document acceptable deviations
with Director/Editor. Verify adjacent views together, not as unrelated beautiful
frames. [H]

For boards/previs mark camera arrows separately from subject arrows, near/far
layers, look targets and panel states. More than one panel may belong to one shot;
number panels under the stable shot ID. The spec, rendered board, timed animatic
and final clip are distinct artifacts. [P16/P17; S02]
