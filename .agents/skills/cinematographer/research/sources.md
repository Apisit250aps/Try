# Cinematographer source registry

Access date for **every accepted entry: 2026-11-25 (Asia/Bangkok)**. Read means
the public text/sections named below were actually opened, not that linked
films, embedded videos, paid books/courses or entire manuals were consumed.
Paraphrases and original exercises only; external film frames/manual pages are
not redistributed in this package. Publication dates are supplied where visible;
undated pages are not assigned invented dates. Claim mappings are in
[notes](notes.md); coverage and proof gaps are in [audit](audit.md).

## C01 — Yale Film Analysis: Part 3, Cinematography

- **Author/type:** Yale Film Analysis teaching resource; university educational
  analysis, individual page author/date not stated.
- **URL/locator:** [Cinematography](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/cinematography/),
  Quality (color/focus), Framing, Scale and Movement public text.
- **Claims:** P01/P05, vocabulary and context-dependent readings; focus/movement
  distinctions. **Limits:** illustrative analysis, not optical engineering or
  experiments demonstrating universal emotions. Lens shorthand is qualified by
  C05/C06; older technology/history descriptions are not copied as current specs.

## C02 — Yale Film Analysis: Part 4, Editing

- **Author/type:** Yale Film Analysis; university teaching analysis, undated.
- **URL/locator:** [Editing](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/editing/), establishing,
  shot/reverse-shot, eyeline/matches, Duration/Rhythm, Continuity Editing.
- **Claims:** P03/P06, spatial/temporal continuity, axis convention, shot relations
  and duration as expressive choices. **Limits:** not a mandate for classical
  continuity or a fixed long-take duration; embedded excerpts were not watched.

## C03 — Yale Film Analysis: Part 2, Mise-en-scene

- **Author/type:** Yale Film Analysis; university teaching analysis, undated.
- **URL/locator:** [Mise-en-scene](https://filmanalysis.yale.edu/mise-en-scene/),
  Lighting and Space sections, including high/low key and three-point lighting.
- **Claims:** P05/P10, lighting vocabulary and staged space. **Limits:** genre
  associations are historical conventions; no racial/moral reading of light or
  universal three-point setup is adopted.

## C05 — Gregory Hollows and Nicholas James / Edmund Optics

- **Title/type:** Understanding Focal Length and Field of View; official optics
  engineering application note, Imaging Resource Guide §1.3, undated.
- **URL/locator:** [Source](https://www.edmundoptics.com/knowledge-center/application-notes/imaging/understanding-focal-length-and-field-of-view/),

## C08 — Bill Holshevnikoff, ARRI Lighting Handbook, fourth edition (2016)

- **Type:** practitioner handbook hosted by the manufacturer.
- **URL/locator:** [Official PDF](https://www.arri.com/resource/blob/83996/409091c612f371b0c68b41d9dcb636db/arri-lighting-handbook-english-data.pdf),
