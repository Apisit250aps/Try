# Claim-to-source traceability

Read this when maintaining or challenging a review principle. S IDs resolve to
the [source registry](../research/sources.md); inspect its exact reading scope
before extending a claim. **E** evidence, **F** critical framework, **H** craft
heuristic, **I** context-dependent interpretation, **T** technical/official
guidance and **D** local system design are different kinds of support.
