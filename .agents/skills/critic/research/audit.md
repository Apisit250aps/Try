## Overlap without ownership transfer

- Storyteller self-checks world/arc; Critic independently tests presented logic
  and disclosure. It diagnoses canon conflicts without authoring canonical fixes.
- Screenwriter diagnoses scenes and revises scripts; Critic questions the claimed
  effect against the actual script/performance, preserving alternate solutions.
- Artist self-checks design; Critic compares selected look and visible artifacts
  with narrative/readability intent. It cannot certify unseen generated images.
- Cinematographer plans shots/coverage and self-reviews; Critic distinguishes plan
  completeness from actual shot/cut effect and returns located concerns.
- Director integrates intent and selects trade-offs; Critic preserves independent
  disagreement. Approval does not erase evidence or establish legal clearance.
- Sound Designer/Editor/Strategist are future owner roles only in this task;
  Critic can name missing specialist evidence without creating those skills.

## Priority gaps and honest gates

1. Actual audiovisual review with inspectable frames **and** moving/audio context;
   include silent animation, performance, joins, sound masking and intentional
   discontinuity. Text pilots or the single-thumbnail C18 pilot cannot close this gate.
2. Fresh human/specialist readings across different aesthetics, languages and
   audience/access needs, preserving disagreements and initial ratings. No
   measured inter-rater reliability follows from one author scoring one executor.
3. Actual multi-specialist handoff and revised artifacts, including a real
   Director contract once present/stable; document mismatches before adapting.
4. Held-out stories, long/episodic scope and adversarial counterexamples to
   distinguish transferable judgment from memorizing the published scenarios.
5. Jurisdiction/use-specific rights, cultural/factual and current delivery
   requirements only when actual project facts require them; no blanket clearance.

No source count, link check, folder structure or pilot score is a production
certificate. The skill can deliver bounded text critique while these gates stay
open. Registry access failures and excerpt limits are retained, not hidden as
“research completed” claims about unread books.
