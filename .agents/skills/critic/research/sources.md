# Critic source registry

Accessed **2026-11-25 (Asia/Bangkok)** for every S entry below. These are actually
opened resources, not a recommended-reading list. Locators identify the portions
inspected; opening a page/PDF does not mean reading the entire work or watching its
embedded films. Summaries are bounded paraphrases; source media/books are not
redistributed. Claims route through [evidence map](../references/evidence-map.md).

Types: **E** empirical research; **F** critical/educational framework;
**H** practitioner heuristic/case account; **T** official standards/guidance.
Local **D** system-design decisions are not established by these sources.
