# Research notes and principle traceability

Read with the [source registry](sources.md). Evidence is mainly practitioner
testimony, educational guidance and studio process descriptions. It supports
informed craft choices; it does **not** establish causal effectiveness through
controlled studies or certify professional mastery.

Labels: **E** inspected observation/testimony; **H** craft heuristic inferred for
use; **C** workflow convention; **T** technical constraint for a named system;
**D** local system design/user requirement. Combined labels do not promote an
inference into empirical evidence. All examples and templates are original.

## Cross-validation and tensions

**Preparation vs discovery (P04/P08).** S01 values thorough rehearsal as a basis
for freedom; S02 describes a less formal approach. S08/S09 describe iterative
discovery before finish. These share an aim of learning, not a fixed procedure.
Choose a rehearsal/rough test that answers the particular uncertainty.

**Vision vs collaboration (P01/P05/P10).** S01 offers a coherent evolving style,
S02 emphasizes creative partners, S15 warns against micromanagement. S03's broad
“boss/final say” language is an educational simplification. S14 distinguishes
creative participation and designated final cutting authority and explicitly
requires a human director under its terms. The AI package therefore supports
creative choices within user authority, without claiming a professional legal role.

**Playable intention vs exact form (P03/P08).** S04 warns about result/line-reading
direction; S08 describes embodied demonstration to animators and S10 stresses
movement craft. Use motivation/task offers for people; demonstrations/pose/timing
can clarify animation, comedy or technical requirements. The distinction is the
purpose and collaborator's workable method, not a ban on precise direction.

**Early sound vs picture-first methods (P06/P07).** S11 argues for early influence;
S06 describes a distinctive constructed-sound workflow. Those are compatible when
sound has an intended role, even if capture/assembly order differs. Do not erase
sound thinking or make full audio a prerequisite for a silent artifact.

**Revisions vs production economy (P08/P13/P15).** Studio iteration and artistic
discovery do not authorize endless generation. The local bounded loop preserves
discovery while requiring a question, allowance and stopping rule. D defaults
remain changeable by the user; missed acceptance stays visible.

**Specificity vs universal emotional effect (P02).** Source accounts can explain
creative intentions and reports, but do not establish all viewers' responses.
Treat emotional journeys as hypotheses; validate in actual artifacts and, when
needed, with intended viewers. No fixed genre/color/shot formula is inferred.
