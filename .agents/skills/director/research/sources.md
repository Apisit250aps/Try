# Director source registry

Accessed **2026-11-25 (Asia/Bangkok)** throughout. “Read” means the identified text
was actually retrieved and inspected, not that a video was watched, a whole book
read or a method experimentally established. Paraphrases only; no source books,
transcripts or media are redistributed. S IDs are local to Director.
