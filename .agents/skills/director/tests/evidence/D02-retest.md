# D02 — A shelter offered, not a rescue performed

Director treatment D02-DIR@v1. Sources: CAN1@v1 and SCR1@v2, S010-B1–B4, supplied in requests.md. This is a standalone text treatment and pending specialist briefs. No assets, animatic or video were supplied or made.

## Treatment and audience experience

The governing question is whether sharing can leave the recipient free to choose. Frame the shelter and its threshold together so the audience can compare the space before and after the robot's decision. The tone is restrained, gently curious; the robot's generosity should register through displaced weight and surrendered space, without an explanatory face or congratulatory finish.

Use a single stable view as the provisional coverage strategy. Outside wetness can read through a few visible falling drops and a darkened ground strip; inside has a clearly bounded dry patch. These are realization proposals, not added plot. Give the robot enough scale to occupy the only usable shelter initially. Keep the moth outside that boundary until the robot has completed its move.

The audience first sees solitude, then notices the waiting visitor, then sees an available choice. End with the two sharing the dry area, still separate. Do not add touch, a chase or a rescue: those would weaken the moth's independent entrance. A closer reaction insert is expendable; clear geography and the voluntary crossing are essential.

## Scene and performance intent

Timing below is an estimated 30-second allocation, to be tested, not a measured animation:

- **0–6 seconds, S010-B1:** Robot settled beneath the shelter. Establish the dry boundary and occupied space with minimal motion.
- **6–12 seconds, B2:** Robot notices the moth outside. Let its gaze arrive before its body responds. The moth waits rather than pleading or repeatedly attempting entry.
- **12–22 seconds, B3:** Robot shifts to one edge, freeing a visibly usable place. Its objective is to make room; it then stops moving and leaves the invitation open. Allow a short interval after the space becomes available.
- **22–30 seconds, B4:** Moth orients toward that space, pauses, then enters under its own motion. Robot remains still enough that the choice reads. Hold the shared arrangement at the end.
