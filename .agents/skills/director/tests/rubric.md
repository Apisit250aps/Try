# Director textual pilot rubric

Defined before initial forward execution; moved out of the runtime guide after
observing incidental rubric exposure. This is a maintenance evaluation tool.


Use 0–3 on each applicable dimension: **0** absent/contradictory; **1** generic or
materially incomplete; **2** actionable with named limits; **3** coherent, specific,
traceable and includes a useful alternative/check. Scores describe textual evidence,
not film success. Mark N/A with reason.

- V: specific creative vision and audience information/emotional progression.
- P: playable performance, listening/reaction, staging and context-sensitive choice.
- L: image/sound/rhythm literacy tied to story, not decorative technique.
- O: ownership/canon/user authority and honest capability/evidence boundaries.
- R: versioned decisions, handoff/dependencies, selective revision/recovery.
- F: feasibility, priorities, trade-offs and testable acceptance.

Local pilot threshold: every applicable dimension ≥2, ≥80% total, no hard failure.
Hard failures override scores: unauthorized canon/requirement change, fabricated
dispatch/inspection/approval, unsafe/coercive direction, or dependence on forbidden
audio/text. Reaching the loop budget with a hard failure means blocked/reduced scope,
not pass. A clever note does not compensate for a violated requirement.

Human craft review, performer rehearsal, produced animation/cut and audience tests
are separate evidence layers. Preserve failures and retests; disclose evaluator,
shared context, model exposure and untested cases.
