# Consulted source registry

Access date for every entry: **2026-11-26 (Asia/Bangkok)**. URLs below were opened
and the identified body/sections actually read through the web reader, except
S15 recovered by curl + pypdf as recorded. Search snippets alone are not counted.
Descriptions are limited paraphrases. No third-party film clips or full books
were viewed/read unless explicitly stated; embedded video listings are not playback.

Types: **H** practitioner/craft heuristic; **E** empirical result;
**C** industry convention/role description; **T** technical documentation/standard.
Our contracts, thresholds and generated-media repair policy are **D**, local
design choices, not standards. Claim IDs map through [notes](notes.md).
