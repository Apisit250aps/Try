# Source registry

Accessed 2026-11-26. Scope below is what was inspected, not a claim to have read
whole books or watched full talks. Source IDs are local; framework and tool behavior
are not empirical proof of audience response.

| ID | Source and author | Inspected locator / supported scope / limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S01 | [Standard Patterns in Choice-Based Games](https://heterogenoustasks.wordpress.com/2015/01/26/standard-patterns-in-choice-based-games/) — Sam Kabo Ashwell (2015) | Pattern definitions, esp. gauntlet, branch-and-bottleneck and sorting hat; practitioner taxonomy with mixed patterns and schematic diagrams, not an exhaustive classification or measured effect. |
| S02 | [Mailbag: High-Agency Narrative Systems](https://emshort.blog/2017/05/25/mailbag-high-agency-narrative-systems/) — Emily Short (2017) | Storylet requirements/results, eligibility, selection, UI and visibility; practitioner design account, not an experiment. |
| S03 | [Beyond Branching: Quality-Based, Salience-Based, and Waypoint Narrative Structures](https://emshort.blog/2016/04/12/beyond-branching-quality-based-and-salience-based-narrative-structures/) — Emily Short (2016) | Opening and quality-based sections: content selection, qualities and storylets; excludes complex simulations from this comparison; no claim of measured superiority. |
| S04 | [Writing with Ink](https://github.com/inkle/ink/blob/master/Documentation/WritingWithInk.md) — inkle | Choice and divert examples, variables/conditional sections; documentation of Ink, not a universal runtime or platform. Version may change. |
| S05 | [Linking Passages](https://twinery.org/reference/en/editing-stories/linking-passages.html) — Twine project | Search engine's captured official page text; direct open returned 403. Passage link syntax and story-format-specific behavior only; no claim of testing Twine. |
| S06 | [Introduction to ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) — Choice of Games | Choice, goto, create/set/if sections: earlier variables affect later scenes; tool-specific syntax, not evaluated by execution. |
| S07 | [Agency Reconsidered](https://eis.ucsc.edu/papers/nwf-C7-digra09-agency.pdf) — Noah Wardrip-Fruin, Michael Mateas, Steven Dow, Serdar Sali (DiGRA 2009) | Abstract and ELIZA discussion pp. 1–3; conceptual analysis of expectations, playable model and agency; not a controlled universal effect. |
| S08 | [Writing & Narrative Design: A Relationship](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2018/presentations/Stirpe_Eric_Writing%20and%20Narrative.pdf) — Eric Stirpe and Molly Maloney, GDC 2018 | Slides 1–3 and conclusion: character/player emphasis and shared story; slides only, audio/talk not watched, a team-specific practice. |
| S09 | [GENEVA](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/geneva-at-ieee-cog.pdf) — Jorge Leandro et al. (2024 revision) | Abstract and introduction/method: GPT-4 prototype, directed acyclic beat graph, four familiar-story case studies; no general creative-quality or production conclusion. |
| S10 | [Using the Cookbook](https://twinery.org/cookbook/using_cookbook.html) — Twine Cookbook | Search result excerpt about Twee examples only; direct open returned 403. No broader Twee claims. |

Murray and Crawford books were not
accessed; S07 discusses Murray's agency concept but is not a reading of her book.
