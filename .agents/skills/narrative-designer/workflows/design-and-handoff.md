# Design and handoff workflow

1. Extract intent, supplied canon/locks, media, audience, mechanisms, deliverables,
   constraints, approval and tool availability. State meaningful unknowns.
2. Develop a concrete narrative premise with Storyteller or provisionally; choose
   linear/interactive approach. Compare alternatives only where a decision matters.
3. Sketch endings and emotional/thematic differences, then routes and choice/state
   effects; choose an architecture and justify cost/agency trade-off. Establish
   event, knowledge and chronology on each route, and visible vs hidden information.
4. Build a small vertical slice before scaling. Specify rule order, invariants,
   default and branches/variants. Cross-check design with the story bible rather
   than copying its authority. Produce requested records using the template.
5. Explore graph and states with available tools, then review creative intent,
   continuity, feasibility and accessibility. Record evidence and gaps separately.
6. Send or prepare route slices for actual owners, mark decisions and approvals
   accurately, plan release and downstream work. On revision, propagate dependency
   invalidation and revalidate; preserve a versioned record.
