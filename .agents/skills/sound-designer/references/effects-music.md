# Effects, spaces and music

Use P01–P04 in [notes](../research/notes.md) as source context. The spotting and
production procedures here are local synthesis, adaptable to the brief.

## Build only meaningful layers

Foley follows performed actions: feet, cloth and handled props. Record material,
weight, gait/effort, contact surface and sync action; a heavy boot on tile is not
interchangeable with a soft shoe on wood. Performance can supply character and
scale. Separate close contact from supporting movement; avoid doubling production
footsteps unless intended. Make safe recording alternatives to hazardous actions.

Ambience establishes a location's larger activity and time/weather. Room tone
maintains the particular interior noise/air signature through dialogue edits.
Record both by location/mic/setup/state when available. A generic wind bed is not
evidence of matching room tone. Check loops for rhythmic recurrence, seams, clicks,
unwanted identifiable voices and shifts in noise/reverb at cuts.

SFX can be library, recorded, synthesized or layered. State attack/body/tail,
material/scale, acoustic perspective, movement, duration and function. Pick one
dominant transient; multiple near-identical impacts can blur timing or cancel.
Retain component IDs, processing order and a rebuildable source trail. “Realistic”
is a creative constraint to define, not a synonym for a louder bass layer.

## Spot music with picture and dialogue

For each music cue specify why it enters and exits, whose interpretation it
supports, diegetic/source or score status, emotional trajectory, protected speech,
hit anchors and how much silence belongs around it. A score may counterpoint
the picture; record the intended reading rather than treating contrast as error.

A composition brief communicates texture/instrument roles, register, density,
pulse/tempo flexibility, harmonic tension/release, motif relationship, resources,
duration/handles, delivery stems and revision constraints. Exact tempo/key only
when the musical need supports it. Describe the function of a temp reference
without asking for a near-copy of melody, arrangement or a performer's identity.
Temp cues remain reference-only and must not enter a release unnoticed.

Keep creative spotting cue IDs separate from music-rights reporting fields.
For a production cue sheet add title, composer/publisher, recording/performer,
usage type, duration and rights evidence; adapt to the actual recipient's form.
Commissioning a score does not itself document all recording/use rights. Use the
[asset registry](../templates/asset-registry.md) for clearance scope and unknowns.

## Make space before processing

When music masks a line, first revise entry/exit, instrumentation/density or
automation at the phrase. Then consider targeted EQ or keyed dynamics while
preserving the intended musical gesture. “Turn everything up” reduces hierarchy.
Check the important word in the full mix, at realistic playback level, in mono
and on intended small speakers. Avoid a universal EQ notch or fixed ducking value
without hearing the actual voice/music combination. A plan cannot certify masking.
