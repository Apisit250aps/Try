# Acceptance and review rubric

Set task-specific criteria before review. Findings identify artifact/version,
cue/scene/shot/range, expected vs observed, consequence, evidence/confidence,
owner, repair and retest. Preserve successful intent and intentional exceptions.
Review is not automatic creative approval or rights clearance.

## Hard gates

- Correct upstream/cut/timebase and stable links; no stale timing represented
  as current, no fabricated files, measurements, listening or receiver approval.
- Rights/voice consent sufficient for the proposed actual use; unresolved assets
  remain blocked from dependent production/release.
- Essential lines/clues and intended silence/reveal order preserved at the
  available evidence level; text cannot establish acoustic intelligibility.
- Measured delivery spec, sync, channel map and required assets/stems/captions
  accepted on actual exports before a production pass is claimed.

Missing applicable evidence is **not-assessed**, not a zero or a pass. N/A means
outside the task. A failed hard gate cannot be averaged away.

## Descriptive dimensions

| Dimension | 0 — contradicted | 1 — partial | 2 — usable at stated evidence level |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Narrative judgment | Generic decoration or unintended reveal | Functions named, perspective/trade-offs unclear | Listening priority, perspective, silence and inference are motivated |
| Voice / intelligibility | Meaning/identity lost or consent bypassed | Direction given, language/timing checks incomplete | Playable direction, text/locale/take links and scoped verification |
| Spatial / serial continuity | Unexplained space/material/motif drift | State recorded inconsistently | Entry/exit, world/listener space and version impacts traceable |
| Technical delivery | Universal target or invented QC | Profile/plan exists but checks incomplete | Receiver spec, reproducible actual logs and scoped listening when media exist |
| Assets / access | Untraceable sources or missing meaningful captions | Registry/notes incomplete | Rights scope, provenance and accessible information survive the handoff |
| Handoff | Unversioned timing or fictitious receipt | IDs exist, adaptation/ownership unclear | Versions, anchors, dependencies, owners and acceptance tests are actionable |

Use dimensions appropriate to the task; do not average into “production ready.”
A text plan may reach 2 for handoff while technical/audio dimensions remain
not-assessed. Self-review, independent Critic review, native-speaker review,
receiver acceptance and audience response must be labeled separately.

## Actual audio exercise protocol

Use authorized reference picture plus separated dialogue, music and effects,
at least one speech/ambience edit, an intentional quiet interval, a sharp sync
event and a locale variant. Retain hashes/versions, tool settings, actual logs
and listening ranges/device notes. Produce only authorized variants. Compare
masking before/after, mono fold-down, mobile playback, lip sync/drift, room
transitions, AI artifacts, post-codec peaks and required stem reconstruction.
Native speakers check locale/pronunciation. Receiver checks the real packet and
returns a located issue or acceptance. Audience comprehension requires listeners;
the designer predicting suspense is not a suspense test. Record failures and
retest the changed evidence without silently overwriting the first result.
