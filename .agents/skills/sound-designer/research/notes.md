## Craft heuristics, not universal laws

Foreground hierarchy, sparse short-form cueing, ADR matching, room-tone joins,
Foley material/effort, motif continuity, masking-first arrangement decisions and
stem reconstruction records are this package's practical synthesis. They should
be adapted and tested against actual work; no EQ curve, compression ratio, emotional
effect, accent quality or universal loudness target is inferred from authority.
The [worked cases](../examples/worked-cases.md) and
[text trials](../tests/text-trials.md) exercise decisions without inventing audio.
