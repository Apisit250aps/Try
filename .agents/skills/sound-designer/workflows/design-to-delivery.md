# Design to delivery

Scale to the task: a script-only concept can stop after a useful spotting plan.
Records below support production; they are not paperwork gates for a tiny note.
See the [contract](../references/production-contract.md) for owners and timing.

1. **Intake and listening intent.** Read the authoritative materials and their
   versions. List what was inspected, available media/tools and what remains
   unknown. Identify audience knowledge, voice/sound/music priorities and limits.
   Use a compact [sound bible](../templates/sound-bible.md) for recurring work.
2. **Spot.** With picture when available, mark cues, intentional absences,
   diegesis/listener, entry/exit reasons and sync anchors in the
   [cue sheet](../templates/cue-sheet.md). In script-only work use descriptive
   anchors and timing estimates; don't invent measured frame positions.
3. **Plan assets and voices.** Read the relevant craft reference, identify needed
   Foley/ambience/SFX/music/voice, and use a [voice profile](../templates/voice-profile.md)
   only where needed. Check [rights and generation](../references/ai-rights-accessibility.md).
   Record actual sources or explicitly absent assets in the
   [registry](../templates/asset-registry.md). Share picture/timing constraints
   with the actual implementer when authorized; otherwise leave a labeled brief.
4. **Produce and edit if possible.** Record/generate/import actual permitted
   sources, retain originals, label takes and select by intent. Compare content,
   pronunciation, noise, room perspective, attacks/tails and sync. Consolidate
   edits without destroying handles; don't replace intentional quiet with a bed.
5. **Conform a revision.** Compare old/new cuts and change lists. Preserve cue
   IDs; update anchors and source offsets, track split/merge/retire mappings.
   Check continuity at changed joins and unaffected-looking downstream cues.
   Mark old audio, music, lip-sync events, captions and QC stale where impacted.
   Timeline changes go through Editor; protected narrative changes through
   Director/writer. Restore validity only after the appropriate recheck.
6. **Premix and final mix.** Establish hierarchy and spaces, then solve actual
   gain/EQ/dynamics/masking/phase issues using
   [engineering guidance](../references/engineering-delivery.md). Use a
   [mix/stems plan](../templates/mix-stems-plan.md) for actual delivery profiles.
   Confirm full mix, stem processing, M&E and locale needs with the receiver.
7. **QC and deliver.** Apply the [rubric](../references/review-rubric.md) at the
   available evidence level. Remeasure exports, audition and check mux/captions
   when tools/media allow; label all unperformed checks. Return artifacts plus
   versions, registry, checks, unresolved exceptions, next owner and acceptance
   request. A creative sign-off cannot stand in for technical/rights acceptance.

For reviews, inspect the actual supplied artifact and give located evidence →
consequence → proposed remedy → acceptance check. For revisions, implement only
authorized changes, preserve accepted work and report dependency impacts. A live
handoff requires an actual receiver response; writing both sides of an example
is an integration walkthrough, not observed collaboration.
