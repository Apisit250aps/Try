# Character, relationships and inhabited worlds

Use for character/world invention and revisions. These decisions supply narrative
facts to Artist, not an appearance sheet. Source IDs resolve in the
[registry](../research/sources.md); diagnostic questions are local synthesis.

## Motives and agency without a personality formula

Build from situation and behavior: what does this person value now, what do they
believe is possible, what can they do, and what happens when they act or refuse?
Differentiate immediate want, longer commitment, fear, self-explanation and actual
behavior. They may align; a hidden “need” is not compulsory. A contradiction is
useful when circumstance makes it legible, not when it is a random trait list.

For a decision, test knowledge + motive + capability + opportunity + cost. If one
is absent, add appropriate evidence, change the event or acknowledge intentional
uncertainty. Do not use an invented diagnosis to make a plot convenient. Interior
complexity and change are distinct dimensions; static does not mean flat ([S02](../research/sources.md#s02)).

Agency means choices affect the relevant experience; it need not mean dominance,
winning, escape or Western individualism. Collective action, acceptance, refusal,
care and constrained choices can matter. If a character is primarily a witness or
patient of events, design what attention, perception or experience organizes the
story instead of secretly assigning them a conventional quest.

Example: a caretaker cannot prevent a building's demolition, but chooses who may
take its plants and what memory to preserve. Counterexample: everyone tells her
what to do and a stranger resolves it, yet the outline calls it her triumph of
agency. Witness-centered or intentionally helpless tragedy could use the latter
events, but would need a different promise and interpretation.

## Arcs and emotional experience

Choose the arc that the premise earns:

| Arc | Observable construction | Cost / failure to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change/growth | Earlier and later comparable choices differ for reasons established by experience. | A speech or new label without changed conduct does not demonstrate change. |
| Disillusionment/decline | A choice gains something while relinquishing a value or damaging a bond. | Do not moralize an identity or illness as a cause of corruption. |
| Steadfast | Values persist while tactics, understanding or the surrounding community changes. | Repetition without new pressure may feel inert; transformation is not the only alternative. |
| Ambivalent/unfinished | Contradictory choices leave a legible unresolved commitment. | Ambiguity must have evidence for plausible readings; missing motivation is not ambiguity. |
| Perceptual/collective | Attention, relationship or communal practice changes without a single heroic conversion. | Do not invent a private trauma to justify every quiet observation. |

Track character feeling separately from desired audience response. A relieved
character may alarm an audience that knows more. At key turns note prior
expectation → evidence/action → revised understanding → likely feeling and its
observable carrier. Intended emotion is a hypothesis, not a measured audience
reaction. Avoid numerical emotional curves that pretend to predict all readers.
Leave room for mixed emotions, reaction and recovery rather than incessant peaks.

## Relationships are reciprocal systems

Give each side a view of the bond: what they want from the other, what they offer,
what they assume, what power they have and what they will not concede. Different
memories can explain asymmetry without making either side a liar. Include affection,
competence, play and care where relevant; conflict alone does not establish a bond.

For each exchange ask what changes in trust, dependence, debt, intimacy, knowledge
or permission. A relationship arc can progress while both personalities stay stable.
Use contrast across repeated situations: once one sibling takes the other's task
without asking; later they ask, accept refusal and adapt.

Example: two siblings both want their father's workshop preserved; one preserves
objects and the other preserves access for apprentices. Their incompatible
methods create action from shared care. Counterexample: one is greedy merely so
the other can teach a moral; a later apology makes the false opposition disappear.
Supporting characters should have a relevant life/interest beyond rewarding the
protagonist. Not every minor character needs a biography or standalone arc.

## Worldbuilding by consequences

Build the part of the world the story uses. Wrede explicitly treats world questions
as selective prompts, not prerequisite homework ([S19](../research/sources.md#s19)).
Record a rule's scope, limit, cost, who can use it, who knows it and demonstrated
exceptions. Test what ordinary people would do with it: work, care, trade, power,
travel, memory and everyday inconvenience. Invent only enough institutions/history
to make relevant consequences credible. A world atlas is not necessary for a
two-minute story at a shared sink.

For story-critical powers, test a cheaper workaround before accepting the obstacle.
If anyone can replay memories perfectly, why trust eyewitnesses and how is privacy
maintained? If a late exception defeats the obstacle, establish its conditions
early or redesign the ending. Intentional inexplicability can serve wonder or horror,
but must not be sold as a solvable mechanical puzzle.

Use [S20](../research/sources.md#s20) for targeted questions on magic limits and
resources/geography, without copying its questionnaire. Separate world fact from
a character's belief, rumor, myth or dream ([S10](../research/sources.md#s10)). Unknown
details may remain unspecified; filling every gap can create accidental canon.

For continuity track elapsed time/travel, ages, relevant geography, object custody,
injury/resources, promises/debts and knowledge at the moment of action. Narrative
memory is not photographic continuity: a story bible can say a key was surrendered;
Artist records the selected key design and visible hand/state in the actual scene.

## Cultural sensitivity and factual grounding

Use these checks when the subject calls for them, not as an unrelated intake
ritual for every invented story. Differentiate an invented practice, researched
fact, contested interpretation and a character's claim. Avoid treating a nation,
faith, disability, class or language group as one personality or a moral symbol.
Distribute relevant agency, ordinary life and internal diversity; test who is
allowed a viewpoint and who exists only as threat, lesson or scenery.

For culturally specific material, determine whose account is being used and what
is public, restricted, sacred or disputed. Consultation can change the premise;
it is not a generic certificate of authenticity. Creative Australia's page
supports cultural authority and collaboration for First Nations Australian
contexts ([S21](../research/sources.md#s21)); do not project its exact protocols
or funding requirements onto another community. Research the relevant community's
own guidance and request missing input when representation depends on it. Do not
claim consultation, consent or lived experience that did not occur.

For a real setting, person, profession or historical event, maintain a small
claim ledger: claim → source/locator/date → confidence or dispute → story use →
fictional alteration. Prefer firsthand/owning institutional records, scholarship
and relevant community accounts; corroborate consequential contested claims.
Do not present imagined private speech, diagnosis or motives as documented fact.
Schaeffer's entry explains why factual truth claims differ from fiction ([S09](../research/sources.md#s09));
it does not replace subject research.

When verification is unavailable, narrow a claim, flag it pending, or propose a
clearly fictional alternative that preserves the intended emotional mechanism.
Do not make up a source, “traditional proverb” or ceremonial detail. Domain-specific
medical/legal/scientific accuracy needs current owning sources or qualified review;
this skill's research is not validation of those fields. Keep the creative work
moving where it does not depend on the unresolved fact.
