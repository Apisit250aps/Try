## Knowledge map

Read the map as dependencies: **brief/experience** feeds **concept + genre +
thematic inquiry**; **characters/relationships ↔ world constraints** generate
**events/associations**; **structure + POV/time/access** arrange the experience;
**information/emotion/pacing** shape its progression; **ending** tests its promises;
**canon/revision/handoff** constrain every stage. Feedback can return upstream.
No stage needs to become a separate agent or required file.

## Integration and overlap decisions

No root shared-runtime contract is added: that would break isolated copying or
require a dependency installer. Each role's contract states its own inputs/outputs;
shared vocabulary is kept consistent. Storyteller records narrative function and
state, Artist records actual appearance/assets. Both may discuss a prop but do
not own duplicate appearance canon. Story-level beats and presentation choices
can be negotiated with Screenwriter without giving Storyteller the shooting script.

The Artist contract's wording is adjusted only to stop describing every named
specialty as future/unimplemented. Its behavior and visual presets remain intact.
README, contributor/installation docs and roadmap discover Storyteller without
claiming a new runtime or changing historic Artist evidence.

## Acceptance boundary

See [evaluation](../tests/evaluation.md) for actual executed cases/checks and
retained outputs. Guidance coverage is not proven expertise. Same-author trials
can expose errors but are weaker than independent blind testing. Future receiver,
audience, timing, large-bible and repeated-model trials remain separate evidence
gates; do not carry their scores forward from packaging success.
