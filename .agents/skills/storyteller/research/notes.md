## What changed decisions

The main synthesis is **experience → organizing interest → motivated events or
meaningful pattern → controlled access → earned consequence → continuity/revision**.
It is deliberately not a mandatory quest pipeline. A small authored story can
use it implicitly; a multi-episode mystery benefits from explicit ledgers.

Frameworks are neither interchangeable nor compulsory. Story Circle tests cost
and changed return; a three-act lens locates consequential turns; the monomyth
can foreground threshold/integration; kishōtenketsu can foreground reframing.
Association and routine need their own legibility tests. The same event sequence
can be told with a different knowledge contract and produce a different intended
experience. These are authorial hypotheses to inspect, not neuroscience claims.

The three records used at production level are related but not merged: world
state, character epistemic state, and audience access. This prevents a retold
event or a recap from being mistaken for something a character has newly learned.
The revision graph extends this distinction to downstream scripts and art packets.

## Evidence strength and remaining research

Scholarly reference entries provide conceptual distinctions, not controlled
trials of story quality. University teaching gives accessible craft exercises,
not a universal curriculum. Creator/foundation accounts describe their frameworks
with advocacy; professional secondary articles can be practical and still make
unsupported universal claims. Publisher chapter overviews establish only the
limited claims actually summarized. Resource landing pages do not substitute
for a whole protocol, and an official video listing does not prove its contents.

No complete paywalled textbook, full curriculum, original-language survey of
East Asian narrative, audience experiment or community consultation was conducted.
No claim of exhaustive narratology, clinical psychology, legal clearance or
cross-cultural mastery is warranted. Genre-romance/comedy/horror rows are local
working heuristics rather than a surveyed field taxonomy; specialist genre work
should add targeted primary/creator/institutional research when relevant.

Local rather than source-prescribed: contracts, file organization, status values,
rubric scores, knowledge/clue tables, rival-solution and deletion tests, role
boundaries, dependency propagation and all invented examples. The source set
changes decisions; its count is not a quality gate. Future research should target
failures observed in actual briefs rather than accumulate unused bibliography.
