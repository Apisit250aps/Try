## CTR and retention diagnosis

Different hypothetical **long-form** upload STR-L01: 10,000 registered thumbnail
impressions, 400 views originating there, 700 total views including external
sources. Correct CTR = 4%, not 7% (S04). Suppose the user additionally supplies a
curve showing an early dip and says the title promises a reveal absent from that
cut. That hypothetical promise mismatch merits a located review, but the numbers
alone don't prove the title caused the dip. Check source/new-viewer mix, lag,
language, actual content and alternate explanations before prescribing a recut.

For low CTR plus strong within-cohort retention, propose truthful packaging
clarity; for high CTR plus weak retention, inspect promise and entry context. If
source mix shifts toward unfamiliar viewers, pooled CTR may fall with healthy
growth. Rewatch spikes need comprehension evidence, not automatic praise.

## Small organic comparison: an inconclusive result

Hypothetical episode A has 30 completions/100 eligible starts; B has 16/40.
Descriptive difference = 40% − 30% = **10 percentage points**, not “10% lift.”
Relative difference would be about 33.3% on A's baseline, but isn't causal lift.
Different topic, release day, paid exposure and returning-viewer mix changed.
Repeated plays may not be independent. Verdict: exploratory and confounded,
precision inadequate to justify a winner; no p-value or invented randomized test.

Next plan STR-EXP01/v1: test whether truthful problem-first packaging improves
qualified viewing without harming comprehension/trust. Native YouTube concurrent
title/thumbnail testing is an option only on a verified eligible long-form account/
format (S27), with its actual watch-time outcome. Shorts alternative: a recruited
randomized prototype study for comprehension, or matched organic observations
labeled noncausal. These study outcomes answer different questions.

independent unit, allocation and sample-size method; all are currently unknown.
Primary: actual design-appropriate viewing/understanding outcome; guardrails:

assignment/missingness. Predeclare window/stop rule and multiplicity handling.
Stop for rights/data/ethical defects; don't stop at the first noisy improvement.
