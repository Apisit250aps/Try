## Cross-validation and contradictions

- **Curiosity vs comprehension/transportation:** S18 suggests an information gap;
  S19 addresses absorption; S21's abstract supplies a processing lens. These are
  different constructs. Design inference: enough context to recognize a question,
  payoff inside the promise, then prototype comprehension. No evidence here
  determines an optimal delay or seconds-long human attention span.
- **Emotion vs channel purpose:** S20 studies transmission, while the project may
  prioritize reassurance/loyalty. Do not optimize news-email virality at the cost
  of a gentle story. Test the intended value and return behavior, not merely shares.
- **Platform views:** S01's explicit 2025 Shorts change and S02's current broader
  starts definition differ from many older tutorials. S13/S14 show Reels drift;
  S10 excludes replays for certain paid events. Preserve product/date/event bases;
  the apparent disagreement must not be “resolved” by a universal view formula.
- **High CTR vs broad distribution:** S04 says audience/source mix affects CTR.
  S27's native test optimizes watch time rather than CTR alone. Joint diagnosis
  is appropriate; no single benchmark determines good packaging or audience fit.
- **Trends vs demand:** S17 warns indexed search isn't polling; S08 supports
  filtered trends; S09 selects ad examples. Triangulating multiple selected feeds
  still doesn't yield a representative population estimate. Independent community
  research or authorized cohorts can supply a different method, when available.
- **Freshness vs authority:** a recent corporate announcement is useful current
  context, but not stronger independent behavioral evidence than an older study.
  Conversely, older academic work cannot establish current product capabilities.
  Match claim type to source type and retain unanswered conflicts.

## Synthesis choices and limits

The skill prioritizes explicit trade-offs, progressive disclosure and small
reversible learning steps. Templates group recurring artifacts rather than
creating a separate file for every strategy term. No framework here asserts
exhaustive competence, optimal score weights, conversion uplift or global audience
preferences. The user's Research-first/Evidence-based/Practice-tested standard is
implemented through consulted sources, bounded claims, worked decisions and actual
offline checks; practice evidence is explicitly separated from real audience data.
