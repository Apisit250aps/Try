## Strategy review / next version

Original goals/budget and version; expected vs measured results; keep/change/park/
stop each series/pillar; contradictions/uncertainty; priorities by goal impact,
confidence, cost and downside; authority and decision source; affected canon/
script/cut/packaging/localization packets, stale dependencies and next owners;
receiver checks/receipt evidence; next observation/review date. No silently moved
goalposts, invented approval, or inference of platform causality from organic lift.
