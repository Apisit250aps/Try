unknown. Explain weight/rank sensitivity; a failed gate cannot be scored away.

## Trend report

Question/decision; exact query/topic vs suggested idea; platform/surface; region/
language/category/filters; observation and retrieval windows/timezones; data source/
definition; comparable baseline; independently corroborating method; examples and
sampling/selection limits; level vs growth; lifecycle hypothesis/confidence;
seasonality/event calendar; creative fit/originality/rights; lead time vs likely
relevance; recommendation and recheck/expiry trigger. If unsupported: “unverified
signal; no live trend data,” then offer a bounded research task or evergreen idea.
