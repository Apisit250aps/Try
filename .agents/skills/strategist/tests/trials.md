work frustration” is a hypothesis, not a discovered demographic. Compare cozy

intended feeling. Don't invent audience size or retention targets. Keep/iterate/
stop at the agreed review point based on comprehension, voluntary return evidence
when available, and actual cost. Use [the launch packet](../examples/launch-series.md).

## T08 — conflicting audience signals

don't estimate the audience preference distribution; repeat-viewing claims also
need cohort/definition/window evidence. Retain gentle positioning provisionally,
log the dark-humor request and seek disconfirming/non-commenter feedback with
neutral questions. If a limited opt-in concept study is feasible, test emotional
promise and comprehension with consent; it doesn't measure feed retention. Director
decides tone changes. Confidence remains low until sampling and cohort context are
known; no simple majority or “audience clearly demands” conclusion.

## T09 — confounded small organic test

Descriptive completion proportions are 30% and 40%, a 10-percentage-point
difference. Different topic/day/music/paid exposure and nonrandom audience allocation
prevent attribution to the variant; repeated plays may reduce independent units.
Call the comparison exploratory/inconclusive, not an A/B win. Before a real trial,
specify baseline/variance, MDE, assignment unit, alpha/power/sample plan, eligible
event bases, guardrails and stop/analysis rule. If unavailable, run a capability/
baseline pilot. No p-value or causal lift fabricated from these totals.
